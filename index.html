Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026

This policy covers the Encode Keyboard Android app and its optional keyboard, published by Nguyen Thanh Dat under the package name com.encodekeyboard.android.

What stays on your device

The app and keyboard convert text on your device. They do not request Internet permission or send typed text, conversion history, codebooks or usage data to the developer. There are no accounts, advertising identifiers, ads or analytics SDKs.

Using the keyboard

If you enable and select Encode Keyboard in Android settings, the app you are typing in receives the text you enter. When you tap Convert, the keyboard reads a limited amount of text near the cursor in the active field and converts the part of the current line before the cursor. The keyboard does not keep a keystroke log or read the clipboard. It clears temporary undo information when the input session ends. Conversion is disabled in fields Android identifies as password fields.

Storage, sharing and deletion

Conversion history from the Create screen, favorites, learning progress, preferences and codebooks are kept in private app storage. Clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app removes them.

Copy places the selected result on the system clipboard. The Share action sends a result to the app you choose. You can import or export JSON codebooks with the Android file picker. Exported files stay in the location you selected until you remove them. Any receiving app or storage provider handles your content under its own privacy policy. You can change the active keyboard at any time in Android settings.

Website and support

This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. Visiting it in a browser is optional. GitHub may process technical request data, including IP addresses, to operate and secure the service. Read GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

If you email support@encodekey.com, the developer receives the email address, message and attachments you send. We use them to respond, resolve support requests and meet applicable legal obligations. You can contact that address to request deletion of your support correspondence.
